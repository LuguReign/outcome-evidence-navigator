03 / METHOD & LIMITS

A review aid, not an evaluation verdict.

01 — INPUT

Public completion reports

Sixteen indicators were manually transcribed from the results tables of two World Bank Implementation Completion and Results Reports. Each record includes a PDF page link and a measurement note.

02 — CHECK

Simple, explicit rules

The application compares actual values with reported current targets, identifies revisions and dropped indicators, and preserves source caveats. It does not infer a target direction for indicators where less could be better.

03 — INTERPRET

Human judgment required

Indicators span outputs, service use, coverage, and quality proxies. Results may reflect revisions, changing populations, and reporting choices. They are not causal estimates, IEG ratings, or WBG Scorecard aggregates.
